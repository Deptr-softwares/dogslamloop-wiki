Privacy Policy

This is a small fan-made wiki run by one person. There's no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and nothing is ever sold or handed to anyone. This page explains the little that does get stored, and why.

If you never log in

Nothing is collected. You can read every page on this site without an account. The site stores a couple of harmless preferences in your own browser (like whether you collapsed the sidebar) — those never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

If you make an account

Accounts exist so contributors can submit edits and get told what happened to them. Signing up stores your email address and a display name. That's it — no real name, no age, no location, nothing else is ever asked for.

If you contribute an edit

Edits are public by nature — that's the point of a wiki. When you submit one, your display name is attached to it and stays attached in the page's history, so readers can see who wrote what and reviewers can discuss it with you. Anything you type into a submission (the edit itself, your changelog note, discussion messages) is stored and visible to the review team.

Withdrawn and declined submissions aren't deleted — they're kept as a record so there's a history of what was discussed and why. Your display name stays on them.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles logins. Your email and content live there.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the site itself. Like any web host, they see request logs including IP addresses. That's ordinary server logging, not something this site reads or keeps.
  • Ko-fi is linked in the sidebar. Clicking it takes you to their site, which has its own policies — nothing about a donation comes back here.

Deleting your data

If you want your account gone, message @deptr4869 on Discord. It's a couple of clicks now, so it'll usually be done the same day.

Your account and email address are deleted permanently, along with any notifications the site had sent you.

Edits you've already made stay on the page, but your name comes off them — they'll show as "Deleted user" instead. They're part of the page's public history, and pulling them back out would break the page for everyone else.

Changes to this page

If any of the above stops being true, this page gets updated. Nothing here is set up to collect more than it says.
