Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Dogslamloop Wiki is a fan-made wiki. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixel, and nothing here is ever sold. This page tells you what does get stored, and why.

Developer and Communication

This site is run mainly by one person, me, under the online alias "Deptr". You can contact me on Discord at @deptr4869.

To be clear: I (Deptr) own and run the site, but its content is written by other (very cool) contributors. A small staff team helps me review edits and moderate discussions.

Making Accounts on the Wiki

Only people who are 13 years old or older may make an account on this site. All it takes is one of these: an email address, or a Discord, GitHub or Google account.

If you are 13, 14 or 15, you need permission from a parent or legal guardian to make an account here.

If an account turns out to belong to someone under 13, it is deleted.

Regarding the data stored here

Reading on the site

  • If you are here only to read, the site collects nothing from you. Reading does not need an account, so read them all. The companies that deliver the pages still see ordinary request logs, like on any website (see "Who else is involved").
  • Your browser keeps a few things for you, on your own device: the combo notation you picked; if you edit, your drafts (hopefully), how you arranged the editor and which tips you have closed; and if you sign in, your sign-in, so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser data removes all of them, which also signs you out and loses your drafts.

Your account

  • Accounts exist so you can make and submit edits, take part in discussions, and have the chance to become a staff member (I hire rarely, so don't come asking for it freely).
  • When you sign up, two things are stored: your email address, which nobody but me can see; and your display name, which everyone can see, because it is the name on your edits, your posts and your profile.
  • Each time you sign in, the address you connected from (your IP address) and the name of your browser are stored with that sign-in, until you sign out on that device. This is part of how signing in works; the site does not use them for anything else.
  • Signing in with Discord, GitHub or Google also stores what that service sends (see "Who else is involved").

Your profile

  • If you add things to your profile, the wiki stores your flair, your bio, and whether you have set your profile to private.
  • Your profile also shows your account role (Reviewer, Trusted Editor, Member and so on), the characters you are a certified expert of, and when you joined.
  • Setting your profile to private hides only your bio. Your flair stays visible.

When you submit an edit

  • Edits are open by nature. When you submit one, your display name goes with it and is shown in the page's history. Readers can see who submitted an edit and which staff member approved it.
  • Everything you write into a submission can be read by the staff team, and by the experts of the character it is for: the edit itself, your QA notes, your display name, any evidence you give, your comments in its discussion, and when it was submitted.
  • Withdrawn and rejected submissions are not deleted. They are kept as a record in the wiki's database, in case one needs to be restored or someone needs to understand why it was rejected or withdrawn.
  • Drafts are saved in your own browser, not on the site. They survive a refresh, but clearing your browser data, or switching to another browser or device, loses them (be careful).

When you upload a media

  • The file, its name, your account and the time you uploaded it are recorded, so the Media Library can credit you under the file and so staff can tell who uploaded what. Anyone signed in can see that credit.
  • Files uploaded before 20 September 2026 have no uploader recorded, and show no credit.
  • Images attached to a discussion post are stored the same way, in a folder under your account, and are public like the post. Deleting your post deletes its images. A post removed by a moderator keeps its images in storage, so it can be put back.

When you engage in a discussion

  • Posts and replies are public. They show under your display name, in the thread or Forum post they were made in.
  • Editing a post replaces its words. Only people with moderator permission can see its earlier versions, and those are deleted when you delete your post or reply.
  • Reporting a post records your account with the report. Only people with moderator permission can see it.
  • If a moderator hides or removes a post, a copy of its words and images is kept in the moderation log, which only people with moderator permission can read.

My Discord server

  • Every character's discussion threads and the Forum are connected to the Dogslamloop Discord server, so a conversation shows in both places.
  • What you post on the wiki is copied to the server within seconds, under your display name. Deleting or editing it on the wiki deletes or edits the copy. Its images are shown there from the wiki's own storage.
  • What is posted in those channels on Discord is copied to the wiki with the Discord display name, handle and account ID of whoever posted it, and its images are copied into the wiki's storage. A message deleted or edited on Discord within a day of being sent is deleted or edited on the wiki too. After that, staff remove it on request.
  • If you signed in with Discord, your Discord account ID is used to check the server's bans and timeouts before your post is copied there, to give you the Discord roles that match your role on the wiki, and to keep your Discord messages off the wiki while your wiki account is restricted.
  • YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS WILL NEVER GO TO DISCORD.

Why the site is allowed to use all this

  • To give you what you asked for: your account, edits, posts, replies, profile, and your role or expertise.
  • To keep the site safe and fair: the moderation system, reports, sign-in records and kept submissions are all for this.
  • Because you chose to: filling in your profile, and signing in with Discord, Google or GitHub. You can take that back at any time by clearing your profile or asking me to delete your account.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase stores the database and handles signing in. Its servers are in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare deliver the site to you, so they see ordinary request logs, such as your IP address.
  • jsDelivr delivers a piece of the site's code to your browser on every page, so it sees the same ordinary request logs.
  • Discord, Google and GitHub handle signing in, if you use them for it. They send the site your email address, your name there, a link to your profile picture there, and your account ID there. GitHub also sends your username. The picture is never shown anywhere on the site.
  • Discord also hosts the connected server, under its own privacy policy.
  • YouTube plays the videos embedded on some pages. A video loads from YouTube when you scroll to it; YouTube sees your IP address, and sets cookies only once you press play.
  • KLIPY hosts the GIFs linked in posts. Your browser loads them straight from KLIPY, which sees your IP address.
  • Ko-fi is a link in the sidebar (please support me!). Nothing comes back to the site from it.

These companies are outside many readers' countries: Supabase's servers are in Germany, and GitHub, Cloudflare, Discord, Google and YouTube are American. So what you give the site may be stored and handled outside the country you live in.

Data Permanence

  • Your account, profile, edits, posts and replies last until your account is deleted (see "Your Rights" for how). Then your account and email address are deleted for good, along with your profile, sign-in records and notifications. Edits, posts and replies stay, but the name on them shows "Deleted user". Copies already on the Discord server keep the name they were sent under.
  • Media you uploaded stays in the Media Library until staff remove it. When your account is deleted, your credit under it is removed.
  • Sign-in records last until you sign out on that device.
  • Submissions are kept as a record, whatever their status.
  • A post's earlier versions last until you delete that post.
  • The moderation log and reports are kept as a record, with the names they were written under.
  • Messages copied from Discord last until they are deleted on either side. A message deleted on Discord is removed from the wiki within about a minute, if it was sent within the last day. An older one stays until staff remove it on request.
  • Browser data lasts until you clear it.

Your Rights (gasps)

  • You can ask me for a copy of your data, and I will send you what the site's database holds about you.
  • You can change your display name, bio and flair yourself, from your profile, as long as you follow common sense and the Forum Rules. If anything else about you is wrong, ask me and I will correct it.
  • You can ask me to delete your account. What stays afterwards is explained under "Data Permanence".
  • You can ask me to stop or limit a use of your data, or take back a choice you made, such as what is on your profile.
  • To ask for any of these, message me on Discord at @deptr4869. I will reply within 2 working days, and do what you asked within 10 working days.
  • If you think I have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this page
