Privacy Policy

Dogslamloop Wiki is a fan-made wiki run by one person. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel, and nothing here is ever sold or handed to anyone. This page tells you the little that does get stored, and why it is stored.

As a general rule of thumb: if you can see it on the site, it is stored. If you cannot, it probably was never collected.

If you are just reading

Nothing is collected. Every page on this site is readable without an account, and you are not asked for anything to read one.

The site does keep a few preferences in your own browser: whether you collapsed the sidebar, which tab you had open, that sort of thing. Those never leave your device. Clearing your browser data removes them, and nothing breaks when it does.

If you make an account

Accounts exist so you can submit edits and be told what happened to them. Signing up stores two things:

  • Your email address: used to sign you in, and nothing else. It is never shown to other readers.
  • Your display name: up to 32 characters, and this one is public. It is the name on your edits.

That is it. No real name, no age, no location, no phone number. None of it is asked for, so none of it can be stored.

Your profile

Everyone with an account gets a profile, and everything on it is optional except the parts the site fills in for you:

  • Description: up to 500 characters, written by you. You can set it to private, and then only you and the staff team can read it.
  • Flair: up to 32 characters, shown beside your name wherever you post.
  • Standing: whether your account is in good standing. Always visible, and not something you set.
  • Characters you are an expert of, if any, and when you joined.

Setting the description to private hides the description. Your name, flair and standing stay visible either way.

If you submit an edit

Edits are public by nature; that is the whole point of a wiki. When you submit one, your display name goes on it and stays on it in the page's history, so readers can see who wrote what and reviewers can talk to you about it.

Everything you type into a submission is stored and readable by the review team: the edit itself, the QA note explaining it, any evidence you attach, and anything you say in the discussion on it.

Withdrawn and rejected submissions are not deleted. They are kept as a record of what was discussed and why, with your display name still on them.

Drafts are the exception. They are saved in your own browser and never reach the site, which also means clearing your browser data loses them.

If you upload a media

The file, its name, your account and the time you uploaded it are recorded, so the Media Library can credit you under the file and so staff can tell who uploaded what. Anyone signed in can see that credit.

Files uploaded before 20 September 2026 have no uploader recorded, and show no credit.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles signing in. Your email and everything you write live there.
  • Discord and Google, if you signed in with one of them. They tell the site your email address and nothing else. Your password stays with them, and this site never sees it.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the site. Like any web host they see request logs, including IP addresses. That is ordinary server logging, not something this site reads, keeps or can search.
  • Ko-fi is linked in the sidebar. Clicking it takes you to their site, which has its own policies, and nothing about a donation comes back here.

Deleting your data

Message @deptr4869 on Discord and ask. It is a couple of clicks on this end, so it is usually done the same day.

Your account and email address are deleted permanently, along with your profile and any notifications the site had sent you.

Edits you already made stay on the page, but your name comes off them and they show as "Deleted user" instead.

Changes to this page

If any of the above stops being true, this page gets rewritten to match. Every version of it is in the site's public repository, so you can see what changed and when.
