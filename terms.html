Terms of Service

Dogslamloop Wiki is a fan-made technical wiki for Jujutsu Shenanigans, run by one person with help from a small staff team. This page is what you agree to by using it.

If you are only here to read, almost none of this applies to you. Most of it is about what happens once you sign in and start writing.

The short version

  • Reading is free and needs no account.
  • What you write stays yours, and you let the wiki publish it, edit it and licence it like everything else here.
  • Follow the Writing Guide. Not following it is how edits get rejected.
  • Be a normal person in discussions.
  • Do not scrape the site. Ask instead.
  • The wiki can be wrong. It is written by players, not by the developers.

Reading the wiki

Every page is readable without an account, and always will be. What you may do with what you read is covered by the content licence. The short version is quote it, screenshot it and link it freely, with credit.

Your account

An account lets you submit edits, upload media and take part in discussions.

  • It is yours alone. Anything submitted from your account is treated as submitted by you, so do not share it.
  • One account per person. Making a second one to get around a restriction is the fastest way to lose both.
  • Your display name is public, and it sits on every edit you make. Names that impersonate staff, the developers, or another contributor get changed.

What is stored and what is not is in the Privacy Policy.

Whatever you write

You keep the copyright to everything you write here. Nothing on this page takes it away from you.

By submitting it, you grant the wiki a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to publish it, edit it, translate it, and licence it onward under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 along with everything else on the site.

You are also confirming it is yours to give: that you wrote it, and did not paste it in from another wiki, a video description, or somebody else's guide. If you did, say so in the QA and credit them.

Withdrawing an edit does not withdraw this grant. Rejected and withdrawn submissions stay in the record, as set out in the Privacy Policy.

Media you upload

The Writing Guide has the full rules on recording and skins. These are the ones that will get a file removed:

  • It has to be yours to upload: your own recording or screenshot. Do not upload somebody else's clip without their say-so.
  • Name it readably. LuckyCoward66dmgCombo.webm, not 10000019283.webm.
  • The site takes webp, webm and gif. Other image formats are converted to webp on upload.
  • Nothing sexual, nothing hateful, nothing gory, nothing off-topic.
  • No troll media. A clip that does not follow the Guide is grounds for rejection on its own.

Uploaded files are credited to your account in the Media Library. Staff can delete any of them, and deletion is permanent.

Editing and review

Every edit goes to the review team before it reaches a live page. They can approve it, reject it, or ask you to change something.

The full Ground for Rejection list is in the Writing Guide. Read it before your first edit rather than after it. Repeated troll submissions get you barred from submitting.

Filling the QA with junk or something unrelated counts as a troll submission.

How to behave

Discussions on this wiki are for working out what a page should say. Disagree with the claim, not the person, and bring evidence.

Hateful language, slurs and discriminatory terms are not allowed anywhere on this site, in an edit or in a discussion.

Things you must not do to the site

  • No automated collection. Scrapers, crawlers that are not a search engine, and bulk downloads are not allowed, whatever the content licence would otherwise let you do with a page. If you want the data, ask.
  • No poking at permissions. Do not try to reach staff tools, other people's drafts, or anything the site did not offer you.
  • Nothing that takes the site down, floods it, or breaks it for other readers.
  • No impersonating staff, experts, or the developers of the game.

Finding a security hole is not breaking this rule. Report it to @deptr4869 on Discord rather than posting it publicly.

Moderation

The staff team can reject an edit, ask for changes, remove media, hide a discussion post, or restrict an account from submitting. A restricted account can still read everything.

If you think a decision was wrong, say so. Message @deptr4869 on Discord, and bring the submission or the page it happened on.

The wiki is provided as it is

This is a hobby project. It can go down, lose a feature for a day, or be wrong about a character. Frame data goes out of date when the game updates, and a matchup rating is an opinion with evidence behind it, not a fact.
