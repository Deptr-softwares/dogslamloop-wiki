Owner and the Wiki

Dogslamloop Wiki is a fan-made wiki for the Roblox game Jujutsu Shenanigans. It is run by one person, Deptr aka me, along with the help of a small staff team handling edits, media, discussion, and the Discord server.

Dogslamloop Wiki is inspired by Dustloop Wiki. It aims to offer readers knowledge regarding the game, strategies and such.

These Terms of Service are the rules for using the wiki. By using it, you agree to them.

The short version

  • Reading is free and needs no account.
  • What you write stays yours, and you let the wiki publish it, edit it and licence it like everything else here.
  • Follow the Writing Guide. Not following it is how edits get rejected.
  • Be a normal person in discussions.
  • Do not scrape the site. Ask instead.
  • The wiki can be wrong. It is written by players, not by the developers.

Account Eligibility

Only people who are 13 years old or older may make an account on this site. All it takes is one of these: an email address, or a Discord, GitHub or Google account.

If you are 13, 14 or 15, you need permission from a parent or legal guardian to make an account here.

If an account turns out to belong to someone under 13, it is deleted.

Your Account

An account lets you submit edits and take part in discussions on the wiki:

  • One account per person. Making another account to get around a ban is not allowed.
  • It is yours only. Anything submitted from your account counts as yours, so do not share it.
  • Your display name is public. People can see it on your profile, your edits and your posts. Impersonating staff or other people may get you banned.
  • Check out the Privacy Policy to see which information is stored about your account.

Permissions and roles are other things an account can have:

  • You can be a Trusted Editor, a Reviewer or an Administrator. Every account is a Member by default.
  • A Viewer is barred from making edits, posting in discussions and the Forum, and uploading media. An account becomes a Viewer when it has 3 strikes. You get a strike when a submission is rejected or when you break the rules, and each strike expires 30 days after it is given. What does and does not count as a strike is under Moderation.
  • Experts are roles you can get. They show your mastery of a character and let you review submissions for that character. For more information, check out #role-information in the Dogslamloop Wiki Discord server.
  • Any account can be given permission to moderate discussions, upload media, delete media from the wiki, and skip the submission cooldown. To get these, message @deptr4869 on Discord.

Media

The Writing Guide has the full details regarding the rules and guidelines when it comes to uploading media. Here's the basic rundown:

  • It has to be yours to upload: your own recording or screenshot. Do not upload somebody else's clip without their permission.
  • Name it readably: for example, LuckyCoward66dmgCombo.webm, not 10000019283.webm.
  • Nothing sexual, nothing hateful, nothing gory, nothing off-topic.
  • No troll media (media that does not follow the Writing Guide).
  • Discussion posts: images attached to a post, and GIFs linked in one, follow the same rules. A post holds up to 4 images, and up to 4 GIFs.
  • Credit and deletion: uploaded files are credited to your account in the Media Library. Staff can delete any of them, and deletion is permanent.

Editing and Review

Every edit goes to the review team before it reaches a live page. They can approve it, reject it, or ask you to change something about it. Read the Writing Guide to know all the Grounds for Rejection and what counts as a troll submission before making your first edit.

Your Behaviour

Hateful language, slurs and discriminatory terms are not allowed anywhere on this site, in an edit, a discussion or the Forum. Do not post or link dangerous files or links (malware, scams, phishing). Do not post other people's personal details, and do not harass people, on the wiki or on Discord.

Discussions on this wiki should stay on topic: about the page or the post they are on.

The Forum is there to create conversation and discussion on the wiki and help it grow. You can post generally anything there, as long as you follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, the Forum Rules, and these Terms.

On the Promotion tag, you are NOT allowed to:

  • spam it;
  • sell anything for real money (selling for Robux is allowed, within Roblox's own rules);
  • scam people;
  • promote other people without their permission.

Any deal made through the Forum is between the people making it. The wiki takes no part in it.

Banned Actions

  • No automated collection. Scrapers, crawlers that are not a search engine, and bulk downloads are not allowed, whatever the content licence would otherwise let you do with a page. If you want the data, ask.
  • No poking at permissions. Do not try to reach staff tools, other people's drafts, or anything the site did not offer you.
  • Nothing that takes the site down, floods it, or breaks it for other readers.
  • No impersonating staff, experts, or the developers of the game.

Finding a security hole is not breaking this rule. Report it to @deptr4869 on Discord rather than posting it publicly.

Moderation

The staff team can reject an edit, ask for changes, remove media, or make an account a Viewer. A Viewer can still read everything.

Staff, and anyone with the moderation permission, can also hide or remove discussion posts, Forum posts and replies. They keep a log of the reasons, and the person who wrote the post gets a notification with the reason.

Strikes

You get 1 strike each time:

  • a submission of yours is rejected;
  • you break these Terms;
  • you break the Forum Rules.

A submission does not give a strike when it is rejected for a grammar mistake, outdated information, an edit conflict, being a duplicate, or being merged by staff. Withdrawing your own submission never gives one.

If an offence is very severe (for example: scams, malware, posting someone's personal details, or sexual content), you instantly get 3 strikes and the Viewer role. Furthermore, your Viewer role could be made permanent, and a permanent Viewer cannot appeal.

Posts that break the rules are hidden or removed.

When a rejection, or a hidden or removed post, gives a strike, the staff note says so. Each strike expires 30 days after it is given. An account with 3 strikes becomes a Viewer.

Appealing as a Viewer

When your account gets the role Viewer, you are stuck with it until you appeal successfully on the Discord server. In order to appeal, you need to wait for all of your strikes to expire. If you get new strikes during this period (for example, a submission you sent before becoming a Viewer gets rejected), you need to wait even longer. Then message anyone in the Discord server with the Moderation Perms role or above, or make a ticket on the Discord server, to start an appeal. If you appeal while you still have strikes, it will be rejected.

If you think any other decision was wrong, message @deptr4869 on Discord, and bring the submission or the page it happened on.

Reports

Signed-in accounts, except Viewers, can report problems with the REPORT button on every discussion post and reply, on pages and in the Forum. For anything else on the wiki, like a page or a piece of media, contact the moderation team on Discord: anyone with the Moderation Perms role or above, or make a ticket. If the problem is a security hole or something more serious, message @deptr4869 instead.

If your work is on the wiki without your permission, report it to the moderation team on Discord with:

  • why it is yours, in your own words;
  • a link to the file or page on the wiki;
  • a link that proves it is yours, for example the original video or post.

The staff team then removes it.

Discord

The wiki is connected to the Dogslamloop Wiki Discord server:

  • Forum posts and replies are copied both ways between the wiki's Forum and the server's forum channel, about every 10 seconds.
  • Messages from Discord show on the wiki under your Discord name. Posts from the wiki show on Discord under your wiki display name.
  • Some things stay on Discord: more than 4 images, files over 8 MB, other file types, forwarded messages and polls. Text longer than 4000 characters is cut off. The wiki copy says what was left behind.
  • Deleting a message or post on Discord removes its copy on the wiki. A Forum post hidden or removed on the wiki is locked on Discord, and unlocked if it is restored.
  • If you signed in to the wiki with Discord, your wiki roles give you the matching roles on the server: Admin of DSL, Reviewer of DSL, Trusted Editor of DSL, Moderation Perms, and the Expert roles. A Viewer loses all of them, and their Discord messages are not copied to the wiki.

The punishments in the Forum Rules are the Discord server's. On the wiki, breaking them gives strikes, as set out under Moderation. The exception is a permanent ban: a permanent ban on Discord means a permanent Viewer on the wiki, and the other way round. When you get one, you get the other (if you have both).
