==Read First==
*All damage values are tested on {{Character Label|GGST|Ramlethal Valentine|label=Ram}} at full health with the R.I.S.C Level at 0 unless the combo cannot work on her, in which case the character with the closest defense modifier is used.
*This list is meant for actual combos, not for frame traps, blockstrings, or mixups. (If the combo itself allows to set them up afterwards, it is mentioned in the "Notes" field). 
*The difficulty is ranked on a scale of 1 to 5 ({{clr|1|Very Easy}}, {{clr|2|Easy}}, {{clr|3|Medium}}, {{clr|4|Hard}} and {{clr|5|Very Hard}}) based on various factors (cancel leniency, link timings, special techniques, range consistency, etc).
*All wall break combos that end with {{MMC|input=214S|label={{clr|S|214S}}}} can be ended with {{MMC|input=623H|label={{clr|H|623H}}}} for more damage. If you would like to spend meter for more damage, keep Shock State, and get hard knockdown after a wall break, use {{MMC|input=236236P|label={{clr|P|236236P}}}}.

This combo is a staple that all Ky players should know, as it applies [[../Strategy#Shock_State|Shock State]] on hit and gives a {{Keyword|Hard Knockdown}}, which can set up for Ky's dangerous {{Keyword|okizeme}} game or give him a {{keyword|meaty}}. In addition, the {{clr|H|2H}} part of this combo can be delayed on block to catch opponents trying to press buttons during Ky's {{clr|S|c.S}} pressure. This in turn opens them up to a throw once they stop mashing buttons, which also gives a meaty attack.

In the midscreen, Ky usually just gets a meaty {{MMC|input=c.S|label={{clr|S|c.S}}}} after {{clr|S|214S}}, but in the corner it becomes much easier for Ky to use {{MMC|input=236H|label={{clr|H|236H}}}} after {{clr|S|214S}} to gain lots of frame advantage on block. ===<span style="visibility:hidden;font-size:0">Close Range Okizeme Ender</span>===
{{TheoryBox
| Title      = Close Range Okizeme Ender
| Oneliner   = When you need a more direct way to start your offense.<br>46-50 Damage (Depending on starter)
| Difficulty = Very Easy
| Anchor     = Ky Beginner Combo 2

A simple yet essential string in any Ky's repertoire, for it grants a {{Keyword|Hard Knockdown}} that starts his okizeme game with {{MMC|input=236H|label={{clr|H|236H}}}} anywhere on the stage. 

Ky's starters into {{clr|D|2D}} include: {{clr|K|5K}}, {{clr|K|2K}}, and {{clr|S|c.S}}. {{clr|K|2K}} is often used in neutral situations due to its balance of range and speed to catch the opponent off guard in neutral with a run up into the move. This works very well as {{clr|K|2K}} has a decent amount of range, is fast, and combos into {{clr|D|2D}} at many distances. {{clr|K|5K}} is more often used during {{keyword|okizeme}} with its infamously long active frames and quick recovery. {{clr|S|c.S}} is used to easily catch escapes with a jump. This combo done with {{Prompt|GGST|K}} buttons also works great as a jump in starter from {{keyword|IAD}} {{clr|H|j.H}} for easy air to ground conversions.

On block, this combo provides many opportunities for catching the opponent off guard during block pressure. For example, using {{clr|K|5K}} with a slight (2f minimum) delay into {{clr|D|2D}} is a 1f (minimum) {{keyword|frame trap}}, which when canceled into {{clr|S|214S}} is a natural 1f double frame trap and safe situation if the opponent is not hit by {{clr|D|2D}}. 

{{ComboText|{{clr|K|2K}} > {{clr|D|2D}}}} into a blocked {{clr|S|214S}} can be used after a blocked [[../Strategy#Shock_State|Shock State]] {{MMC|input=214K|label={{clr|K|214K}}}} for a {{Keyword|Frame Trap}}, but it also combos if the opponent is hit by the SS {{clr|K|214K}}.

The goal with combos is to end them in a way that gets you some kind of advantage beyond just damage. You can end combos into a knockdown, or corner carry to put yourself in a better position. * ...(airborne opponent) > {{clr|H|5H/2H}} > {{clr|K|214K}}
** Large amount of corner carry.
* ... > {{clr|H|632146H}} / {{clr|P|236236P}}

| content    = * Pre DI: Shock State combo filler, wall breaker.

In DI, {{clr|H|H}} Vapor Thrust is significantly buffed. It pops the opponent very high on hit, even on a normal ground hit. Links into {{clr|S|c.S}} on hit even midscreen. However, the time to confirm it from a midscreen hit quite tight, only allowing 1 to 2 hits before {{clr|H|623H}} must be used. Other than combo filler, DI {{clr|H|623H}} has a less often seen use: {{clr|H|j.623H}} can be used to convert air hits into a full grounded combo. This must be done high enough in the air to gain enough plus frames to strike the opponent before they reach the ground.

{{clr|H|623H}} is normally Ky's highest damage wall breaker, but in DI the total damage is spread out between 3 hits, making it a poor corner combo ender. Instead, use {{clr|D|5[D]}} when close and with enough time to charge it or use {{clr|H|6H}} as a quicker and farther reaching substitute.

==Extra Combo Theory==
=== {{clr|D|5[D]}} Pickup ===
After Red Roman Cancel and also certain counterhits (such as CH {{clr|S|c.S}}), Ky can use a fully charged {{clr|D|5[D]}} to launch the opponent higher up into the air, resulting in higher damage combo extensions. The easiest route after {{clr|D|5[D]}} popup is {{ComboText| dash {{clr|S|c.S}} > {{clr|S|2S}} > {{clr|H|5H}} > (...)}}, but a deep dash-in after {{clr|D|5[D]}} into air hit {{ComboText|{{clr|S|c.S}} dash cancel {{clr|S|c.S}} > (...)}} routes deals optimal damage and have a lot of corner carry, but can be tricky to land.

This works anywhere on the screen (except the corner when used off CH {{clr|S|c.S}} instead of RRC) and is consistent against all characters. It will also work if the opponent is launched high enough in any other combo route.

==={{clr|H|2H}} > Foudre Arc===
{{clr|H|2H (2)}} universally combos into {{clr|K|214K}} against all grounded opponents if they are in Shock State

If {{clr|K|214K}} hit your opponent while they were in Shock State, you can usually link {{clr|K|5K}} or {{clr|K|2K}} and cancel into Stun Dipper ({{clr|K|236K}}) to end the combo, depending on the character and spacing ({{clr|K|2K}} can more reliable since it reaches further than {{clr|K|5K}}). The {{clr|K|2K}} link works on everyone except for I-No. She requires you to be point blank with your {{clr|H|2H}}, otherwise {{clr|K|2K}} won't reach. On the larger characters like {{Character Label|GGST|Faust}}, {{Character Label|GGST|Potemkin}}, {{Character Label|GGST|Leo Whitefang}}, and {{Character Label|GGST|Nagoriyuki}}, you can instead end the combo with Dire Eclat ({{clr|S|214S}}) instead of {{clr|K|236K}}, since their hurtboxes are bigger than the other characters on whom max range {{clr|K|2K}} > {{clr|S|214S}} will not work. This will reapply Shock State, making it preferable over Stun Dipper for these characters. If near the corner, Ky can opt for {{clr|K|2K}} > {{clr|H|6H}} -> special cancel to secure a wallbreak.

===Lightning Strike combos===
While Lightning Strike usually requires meter to combo from due to Ky's high recovery time making a Roman Cancel necessary, he can use the slowdown from close-range big counterhits to cover that recovery period and give him enough time to act before the opponent reaches the ground. This will typically be useful at midscreen from near point-blank counterhits with {{clr|H|6H}} or {{clr|D|2D}}, where {{clr|S|236S}}~{{clr|P|4P}} will launch high enough during slowdown for Ky to recover in time to pick up reliably with {{clr|H|5H}} or dash {{clr|K|5K}}/{{clr|K|2K}}. 

Since Lightning Strike will always hit after Stun Edge in this scenario, it will always leave the opponent in Shock State. This allows Ky to use {{clr|H|623H}} to send the opponent close to the corner at around round-start position, making it a strong option to either keep the corner or break the wall without resources, where Ky would otherwise need to spend meter to achieve this.

While the same kind of routing can be used in the corner, it's usually going to deal less damage than typical meterless corner routing like {{ComboText| {{clr|H|6H}} > {{clr|S|214S}} > dash {{clr|K|2K}} > {{clr|H|6H}} }} etc

====Kara Lightning Strike====
Ky can additionally use the startup of {{clr|P|214P}} to kara cancel into supers, enabling unique combo routing such as using {{clr|H|236H}} juggle routing kara cancelled into Dragon Install for a full DI combo, a {{clr|S|236S}} hit kara cancelled into Sacred Edge for the bonus Shock State damage, or even more easily using un-stick routing off {{clr|H|6H}} wallbounce to extend a combo via enhanced Sacred Edge without breaking the wall. It also allows Ky to do his highest damage wallbreak rather than Sacred Edge; with the opponent wallsplatted and Ky spaced from the wall, kara {{clr|H|236H}} into RTL such that every hit of {{clr|H|236H}} connects while the opponent is being hit by RTL. This provides a small damage boost that could be necessary to confirm a kill.
