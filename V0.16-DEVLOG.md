## Already done, so NOT carried

Worth stating explicitly, because an inventory that still lists finished work
sends somebody to build it twice.

* **Fine-tuning 3 — `tier-editor.html`.** Pulled forward into v0.15 at the
  owner's request: the people assigned to tier lists could not do their jobs in
  it. Preview panel, scroll region, tier-name length and the v0.14 colour picker
  all shipped.
* **Fine-tuning 7 — stop auto-filling the Author field.** Pulled forward into
  v0.15's bug batch, because a combo card made the credit unreachable rather
  than merely pre-filled. `spawnBlockWithAuthor` no longer stamps any block
  type, and the card editor exposes the field so an existing credit can be
  cleared.
* **Bug 4 — merged tickets break Diff View.** Fixed in v0.15 item 6.

## Bugs still open

Numbered as they were in v0.15's list, so the two files agree.

1. **Editing scrolls to the top.** Lists and very long paragraphs, in Firefox
   and Edge, sometimes jump the workspace back to the top mid-edit.
2. **Intercept-and-edit does not work** on your own submission in
   `submission.html`. Suspected in the admin scripts' intercept logic.
3. **A character state is silently dropped on submit.** Three states edited,
   two submitted, one gone without a message.
5. **Switching views always lands on Overview**, whichever tab you were on.
   Still unverified — nobody has reproduced it deliberately.
6. **"Edit this tab" from an ultimate tab** opens the base character's Overview
   instead of that tab.
