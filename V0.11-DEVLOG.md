## Scope: Discoverability & Accessibility

Per the roadmap. **Confirm scope is still current before starting** — see `feedback_confirm_scope_before_each_roadmap_phase` in memory.

1. **Accessibility baseline.** Character pages have no `<h1>`, and there is zero `aria-*`, `role` or `tabindex` anywhere in the codebase. Now cheaper than it was: the page router (v0.9) means one skeleton change reaches all 30 generated pages at once.
2. **`sitemap.xml`** — generated from `site_pages`, so it belongs alongside the existing generators rather than being hand-maintained.
3. **`robots.txt`.**

OG/social meta already shipped in v0.9, and character portraits in v0.10, so the SEO half of this version's original scope is largely done.
